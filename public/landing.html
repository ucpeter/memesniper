What is actually in the box

Features that exist in the code

Not a feature list copied from a marketing page — every item below is implemented, tested, and documented in the project README.

📉

Trailing stop

Activates at a gain you set, then ratchets up with the peak and never loosens. The peak is preserved across a restart, so a long hold keeps the gains it already earned.

🛡️

Hard loss limit

Per trade and per day. Hit the daily number and that wallet stops opening anything new — the circuit breaker is per wallet, not a global panic that takes the others down with it.

🎯

Partial sells

Sell X% at +Y%, in as many tiers as you like, highest threshold first. Sizes are absolute token amounts, so several tiers firing in one tick cannot undersell each other.

👛

A strategy per wallet

Each wallet carries its own sizing, exits, limits, filters and AI settings. One can scalp tight while another holds for a large target. They do not share anything.

🌊

Liquidity range

Snipe tokens whose bonding curve holds between a floor and a ceiling of real SOL. Because that number grows as others buy, it doubles as a timing control: how early you are willing to enter.

🔬

Rug checks before the buy

Mint and freeze authority, top-holder concentration, dev holdings, bonding-curve progress, metadata and socials. Hard fails block the trade; unknown data never does.

🤖

Optional AI veto

Bring your own key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI) and the model gets a yes/no veto over every candidate. Leave it off and nothing changes. It fails open, and it never moves money.

💸

Fund and withdraw in-app

Connect your own wallet, approve a transfer, and the burner is funded. Withdraw all of it or an amount you type — to your own address, never to one of ours.

⛔

Kill switches that work

Kill one position, kill a wallet (and stop it), or panic everything. They market-sell at any price and report honestly if they could not.

From nothing to funded

How it works

Four steps, all inside the Terminal. No account, no KYC, no deposit address.

  1. Set a passphraseThe keystore is created on your machine and encrypted with AES-256-GCM. It locks itself on every restart, by design.
  2. Create a burner walletA throwaway hot wallet the bot generates and holds the key for. Name it. Give it its own strategy: exits, limits, filters.
  3. Fund it from your own walletConnect Phantom, Solflare, Backpack or Magic Eden, type an amount, approve the transfer. There is no address of ours to send to — ever.
  4. Start it and watchPress Start on the wallet. Dry-run is on until you deliberately turn it off, twice.
Questions worth asking

FAQ

Does closing the browser tab stop the bot?

No. The engine runs as a process on your machine and the browser is only a remote control over a WebSocket. Measured on this build: with the dashboard closed, the scanner kept detecting launches and the price feed stayed healthy. Exits do not need a tab to be open.

What happens if the bot restarts while I hold a 24-hour position?

Open positions are written to data/positions.json. When the bot comes back and the keystore is unlocked, each one is checked against the wallet's real on-chain balance and re-adopted — with its cost basis and its trailing-stop peak intact. If a balance read fails, the position is kept for the next attempt rather than forgotten, because unknown is not zero.

Why is the keystore locked? I never locked it.

Because it locks itself on every restart. Your keys live in memory only while running and are never written to disk unencrypted, so a fresh boot always starts locked. That is a safety feature, not a fault. Unlock once and it stays unlocked until the next restart.

Can the bot take my main wallet?

No. It never asks for your seed phrase, and the only thing it ever asks your connected wallet to sign is a transfer it authored, which the server verifies instruction-by-instruction before broadcasting: program, source, destination, amount and payer must all match the intent you approved. signAllTransactions and signMessage appear nowhere in the codebase.

What do I need to run it?

Node 20 and a Solana RPC endpoint. The public one works but rate-limits hard; a free Helius or QuickNode key makes a large difference. A full deployment guide for running it locally and on Render is in the README.

Is it audited?

No, and anyone who says otherwise about their own bot is selling something. It ships with 99 tests, the money-handling paths are covered, and the source is short enough to read. Treat it as a tool you are responsible for, not a product you are insured against.
